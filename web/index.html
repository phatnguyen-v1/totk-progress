Set up TotK Progress Map

Point the app at your TotK Recomp folder: the one with totk.exe. It reads your saves from there and updates the map whenever the game saves. Nothing is ever written to your game or saves.

2. Map and quest data

The first time, the app draws the map and finds every shrine, Korok, cave and quest in your own TotK Recomp game files (about a minute, 20 MB). Nothing is downloaded. Everything is stored in .

Language

The app, and the place, item and quest names and quest texts, are shown in this language. Names and quest texts come from your game files. Same as the game follows TotK Recomp's language setting. The app's own translations are drafts: corrections are welcome on GitHub.
